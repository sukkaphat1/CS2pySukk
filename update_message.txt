v1.0.7 - Per-weapon triggerbot tap fire.

Set the Tap Fire Interval slider, then click "Save Tap Time for Current Weapon" while holding that gun, and enable "Use Per-Weapon Tap Times".

Saved times apply per weapon in the background - the slider won't change and stays as the fallback for weapons you haven't saved.
